    A QUICK PLAY BREAK

    Pick a corner.
    Back your choice.

    1. Choose left, centre or right.
    2. Press Shoot. The keeper picks a direction independently.
    3. A matching dive is a save; any other dive is a goal.
    4. Take five shots, then play again for a fresh score.

    The little twist
    The keeper's choice is random. Changing your aim does not tell the keeper where to dive.

    Use touch or a mouse, or move between the buttons with Tab and activate them with Enter or Space.

      READY WHEN YOU ARE

      KEEP YOUR EYES ON THE FREE PLAYER

      Press Start, wait for the highlighted teammate, then select them. An early, wrong or late pass uses one attempt.

      Five passes, one at a time. Start when you are ready.

      PLAY THE OPEN PASS

      A little patience.
      A quick decision.

      1. Press Start. A short, random wait begins.
      2. One teammate lights up. Pass to that player within three seconds.
      3. Read your reaction time, then choose Next pass.
      4. After five attempts, see your hit count and average successful reaction time.

      Keep it honest
      Passing before the signal, choosing the wrong player or waiting too long counts as a miss.

      Use touch, a mouse or Tab and Enter. While focused in this challenge, keys 1, 2 and 3 select the matching teammate. Switching to another tab pauses an unfinished round.
