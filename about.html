03 / BEHIND THE PROJECT

A student.
A ball. An idea.

A small personal project about making room for something I enjoy.

Hi, I am Lian zhihan.

I am a Computer Science student at BiUH. I enjoy playing football and billiards. Campus Football Lab brings my interest in football together with the HTML, CSS and JavaScript I am learning.

This guide is for students who want to practise a little between classes, whether they have a friend available or just a ball and some space. The aim is simple: fewer decisions before you start, and a clear thing to work on.

Small enough to be useful.

I chose four pages with different jobs: an introduction, a readable drill library, a session builder and this explanation. You can move between them using ordinary links. The builder combines drills and divides the available minutes; it does not need an account or a database.

Forest green comes from the pitch. Lime marks the places to act. The diagrams, icons and social cover were drawn for this project, so they share the same visual language.

How the content was made.

The eight short setups and their durations are original examples for casual practice. The FIFA Training Centre articles below informed general ideas such as scanning, controlling the ball and preparing a first touch. These are independent adaptations, not copies of the full FIFA sessions.

AI assistance and authorship

AI assisted with planning, the first version of the code, English wording, original vector artwork and testing. I need to review the content, understand the code and explain the decisions in my own words. The repository preserves real dated changes as the project develops.
