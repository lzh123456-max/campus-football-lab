# Campus Football Lab

## The first scope — 8 November 2026

A small English-language football practice guide for students who want something useful to do with a ball between classes. The owner is Lian zhihan, a Computer Science student at BiUH who enjoys football and billiards. This is an independent student project, not an official university club or a professional coaching service.

The title has three words. The visual identity uses forest green, warm white and lime, with original pitch illustrations. The first poster and matching icons are made before the page layout.

## Four pages

1. **Home — index.html:** the audience, an original football illustration and clear routes into the site.
2. **Drill library — drills.html:** eight fully written practice ideas with steps, equipment, player counts and category filters.
3. **Session builder — session.html:** choose a focus, available time and solo/partner practice; generate a usable timed plan and track its steps.
4. **About — about.html:** the author, design decisions, source credits and a clear account of AI assistance.

The page that must work well is the session builder. The smallest useful version is four complete pages, a searchable practice collection and a plan whose time adds up correctly. Practice ideas and durations are original examples for casual practice, informed by the credited technical concepts. They are not reproductions of FIFA sessions.

## Technical scope

Semantic HTML, one shared stylesheet and small plain JavaScript files. No accounts, backend, database, cookies, remote scripts or storage dependencies. Navigation must still work without JavaScript. The library remains readable without JavaScript; filters and the session builder progressively add interaction. All assets are served with the site, including icons and the social image.
