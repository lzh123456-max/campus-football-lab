01 / PASSINGWith a friend

Gate passes

Practise controlled passes through a small gate with a partner.

Try it for
5 minutes
Space
A clear 6 × 4 m area
Bring
1 ball and 2 cones
View practice steps +
  1. Make a one-metre gate; stand opposite your partner, three metres away.
  2. Pass through the gate, then settle the return before passing again.
  3. Alternate feet and adjust your distance to keep the passes controlled.

Keep in mind: Look up; give your partner a manageable ball.

02 / PASSINGSolo

Wall return

Use gentle rebounds to practise passing with both feet.

Try it for
4 minutes
Space
A permitted wall with a clear run-off
Bring
1 ball and a suitable wall
View practice steps +
  1. Choose a solid wall away from windows, doors and other people.
  2. Stand two metres back, pass gently and control the returning ball.
  3. Alternate feet; pause to retrieve any loose rebound.

Keep in mind: Keep the rebound easy to receive rather than increasing power.

03 / DRIBBLINGSolo

Cone weave

Follow a short cone line while keeping the ball nearby.

Try it for
5 minutes
Space
A clear 8 × 3 m strip
Bring
1 ball and 4 cones
View practice steps +
  1. Place four cones in a line, about one metre apart.
  2. Weave slowly between them using small touches with either foot.
  3. Turn and return; widen the gaps if needed.

Keep in mind: Glance ahead between touches so the next gap stays visible.

04 / DRIBBLINGSolo

Figure eight

Explore changing direction on a figure-eight route around two markers.

Try it for
4 minutes
Space
A clear 5 × 4 m area
Bring
1 ball and 2 cones
View practice steps +
  1. Place two cones two metres apart with room around them.
  2. Dribble around each cone in turn, crossing through the middle.
  3. Reverse your route halfway through and try using your other foot.

Keep in mind: Use short touches around each curve; speed can come later.

05 / FIRST TOUCHWith a friend

Receive and turn

Guide your first touch before returning the ball.

Try it for
6 minutes
Space
A clear 6 × 5 m area
Bring
1 ball and 2 cones
View practice steps +
  1. Stand three metres apart with a cone beside each receiver.
  2. Receive a gentle pass and guide it around your cone.
  3. Turn back, return the pass and switch the receiving foot regularly.

Keep in mind: Check the open space before the ball reaches your feet.

06 / FIRST TOUCHSolo

Wall first touch

Use a wall rebound to practise receiving sideways.

Try it for
5 minutes
Space
Permitted wall with 4 m clear space
Bring
1 ball, 2 cones and suitable wall
View practice steps +
  1. Mark a receiving area two metres from a suitable clear wall.
  2. Pass gently, then guide the rebound left with your first touch.
  3. Reset in the middle and repeat to the right, alternating feet.

Keep in mind: Angle your body towards the space you want to use.

07 / FINISHINGSolo

Target finish

Place a controlled finish after one small setup touch.

Try it for
5 minutes
Space
A clear pitch with a safe target
Bring
1 ball and 2 cones
View practice steps +
  1. Mark a wide target in an empty goal or clear run-off.
  2. Start four metres away and nudge the ball into your path.
  3. Place a low shot through the target, retrieve it and alternate feet.

Keep in mind: Set up a comfortable next step before choosing your finish.

08 / FINISHINGWith a friend

Pass and finish

Connect a pass, a useful first touch and a finish.

Try it for
6 minutes
Space
A clear pitch with a safe target
Bring
1 ball and 2 cones
View practice steps +
  1. Mark a wide target; your partner stands safely beside you.
  2. Ask for a gentle sideways pass into your clear shooting lane.
  3. Take a setup touch, finish low, then retrieve and swap roles.

Keep in mind: Agree the passing direction before starting; keep the lane clear.
